Food Guide

Where to Eat

Two iconic food districts, each with a story on every plate.

🌆 Karol Bagh

A bustling urban neighbourhood famed for its diverse food scene — from parathas at sunrise to chaat in the evening rush.

Famous for
Puri Sabzi Aloo Tikki Chole Bhature Jalebi Gol Gappe

🕌 Chandni Chowk

One of India's oldest and busiest markets, with lanes that have fed empires. A pilgrimage for any food lover.

Famous for
Paratha Wali Gali Daulat Ki Chaat Kachori Kulfi Faluda Biryani
